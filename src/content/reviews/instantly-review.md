Cold email outreach remains one of the highest-ROI channels for B2B lead generation, but technical hurdles like email deliverability, domain warmups, and strict inbox sending limits make scaling a challenge. **Instantly.ai** was built specifically to solve these problems by allowing revenue teams to connect unlimited sending accounts and automate high-volume outreach without burning domain reputations.

## What is Instantly.ai?

Instantly.ai is a specialized cold email platform designed to scale outbound campaigns through unlimited inbox warmup, automated sending account rotation, and integrated lead databases. Rather than sending thousands of emails from a single address, Instantly distributes campaign volume across dozens of separate domains and sending accounts to ensure emails hit the primary inbox.

---

## Key Features

### 1. Unlimited Account Warmup Network
Sending cold emails from a new domain without proper warmup guarantees high bounce rates and spam folder placement. Instantly provides a native, automated warmup network consisting of hundreds of thousands of real accounts that exchange, open, and mark emails as important to establish strong sender domain reputation.

### 2. Multi-Account Rotation
To stay within daily sending limits imposed by Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 (typically 30–50 emails per address per day for cold outreach), Instantly allows you to attach unlimited sending accounts to a single campaign. The system automatically rotates sends evenly across all connected accounts.

### 3. Unified Inbox (Unibox)
Managing replies across 20+ separate cold email accounts can quickly become chaotic. Instantly’s **Unibox** aggregates all incoming responses into a single master inbox, allowing sales representatives to respond to prospects, update lead statuses, and book meetings from one central interface.

### 4. B2B Lead Database & AI Copywriting
Beyond campaign execution, Instantly includes a built-in search database with over 160 million B2B contacts, as well as AI-powered messaging features to generate personalized subject lines, email bodies, and follow-up sequences based on prospect data points.
