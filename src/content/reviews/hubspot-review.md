# HubSpot CRM Review (2026)

HubSpot is one of the most popular CRM platforms for growing businesses. In this review, we examine its core features, pricing plans, and overall usability.

## Key Features
- **Free CRM Tools:** Unlimited users and contact management.
- **Sales Hub:** Pipeline management and email tracking.
- **Automation:** Workflows and sequence automation for sales teams.

## Conclusion
HubSpot remains a solid choice for startups and growing SMBs looking for an all-in-one CRM solution.
