# Fathom AI Review (2026): Is the Free AI Meeting Assistant Worth It?

Managing back-to-back video calls while taking manual notes is inefficient and leads to missed action items. **Fathom AI** (fathom.video) has disrupted the AI note-taker landscape by providing free, unlimited meeting recording, real-time transcription, and automated AI summary generation.

In this review, we examine Fathom AI's core capabilities, free vs. paid tier limits, CRM integration efficiency, and how it stacks up against paid alternatives like Fireflies.ai and Otter.ai.

## What is Fathom AI?

Fathom AI is an intelligent meeting assistant that joins your video calls on **Google Meet, Zoom, or Microsoft Teams**. It automatically records, transcribes, highlights key moments, and generates formatted meeting summaries within seconds after your call ends.

Unlike many competitors that lock core features behind steep monthly subscriptions, Fathom provides its robust individual note-taking engine for free—making it a favorite among remote founders, sales reps, and growth marketers.

---

## Key Features

### 1. Free Unlimited Recording & AI Summaries
The standout feature of Fathom is its free tier. Individual users get access to **unlimited call recording and AI summary generation** without strict monthly task caps or hidden time limits on call length.

### 2. One-Click Meeting Highlights
During live calls, you can click a button or use a keyboard shortcut to flag important moments. Fathom creates video clips ("highlights") of those exact seconds, allowing you to easily share key decision points or customer feedback with team members.

### 3. Native CRM & App Integrations
Fathom automatically syncs formatted meeting summaries and action items into **HubSpot, Salesforce, and Close CRM**. You can also route summaries to Slack channels, Notion databases, or Zapier/Make/n8n webhooks.

### 4. Enterprise-Grade Security & Privacy
Fathom utilizes end-to-end encryption for stored recordings and complies with SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, and HIPAA standards, ensuring enterprise data remains private and secure.
